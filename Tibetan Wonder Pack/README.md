# Tibetan Wonder Pack

A comprehensive Civilization V mod featuring 8 authentic Tibetan wonders spanning from Ancient to Industrial eras, each with unique mechanics that reflect Tibet's rich cultural and religious heritage.

## Overview

The Tibetan Wonder Pack adds eight carefully researched and historically accurate wonders to Civilization V, each representing important landmarks, religious sites, and cultural institutions from Tibetan history. These wonders feature unique gameplay mechanics that go beyond simple yield bonuses, offering strategic depth and cultural authenticity.

## Features

### 8 New Wonders Across All Eras

**Ancient Era**
- **Yumbu Lagang** - Tibet's legendary first palace, providing mountain-based defensive bonuses

**Classical Era**
- **Jokhang Temple** - Sacred heart of Tibetan Buddhism, rewarding cultural diversity
- **Samye Monastery** - First Buddhist monastery in Tibet, enhancing religious unit mobility

**Medieval Era**
- **Sakya Great Library** - Renowned center of learning with Great Work storage

**Renaissance Era**
- **Potala Palace** - Iconic palace of the Dalai Lamas with faith-based defense
- **Norbulingka** - Summer palace with enhanced garden mechanics
- **Gyantse Kumbum** - Artistic wonder with unique theming bonuses

**Industrial Era**
- **Dégé Parkhang** - Historic printing house that spreads religious influence through literature

### Unique Mechanics

Each wonder introduces innovative gameplay mechanics:

- **Mountain Defense Systems** - Mountains provide defensive bonuses to nearby cities
- **Cultural Exchange Bonuses** - Foreign Great Works provide additional benefits
- **Enhanced Religious Units** - Increased movement for missionaries and religious units
- **Faith-Based Defense** - City defense scales with religious population
- **Garden Synergy** - Doubled effectiveness of Garden buildings
- **Dynamic Theming** - Era-diverse art collections provide enhanced bonuses
- **Literature-Based Pressure** - Great Works of Writing increase religious influence range

## Wonder Details

### Yumbu Lagang (Ancient Era)
- **Cost**: 185 Production
- **Tech Required**: Animal Husbandry + Masonry
- **Location**: Must be built on Mountain
- **Effects**: 
  - Mountain tiles provide Defense Points to Cities
  - Defense Buildings provide +1 Faith
  - +500 City Defense
  - +1 Great Engineer Points

### Jokhang Temple (Classical Era)
- **Cost**: 250 Production
- **Tech Required**: Philosophy
- **Effects**:
  - +1 Happiness, +1 Culture
  - Great Works from other Civilizations provide +3 Faith
  - +1 Great Artist Points

### Samye Monastery (Classical Era)
- **Cost**: 300 Production
- **Tech Required**: Theology
- **Effects**:
  - +2 Faith
  - Religious Units gain +1 Movement
  - +2 Great Engineer Points (representing architectural innovation)

### Sakya Great Library (Medieval Era)
- **Cost**: 400 Production
- **Tech Required**: Education
- **Effects**:
  - +2 Science
  - +2 Great Scientist Points
  - 2 Great Work of Writing slots

### Potala Palace (Renaissance Era)
- **Cost**: 625 Production
- **Tech Required**: Astronomy
- **Location**: Must be built on Mountain
- **Effects**:
  - Defense Points for each religious follower in Cities
  - +3 Great Engineer Points
  - +500 City Defense

### Norbulingka (Renaissance Era)
- **Cost**: 625 Production
- **Tech Required**: Banking
- **Location**: Must be built next to River
- **Effects**:
  - +3 Happiness
  - Gardens have doubled efficacy
  - +1 Great Artist Points

### Gyantse Kumbum (Renaissance Era)
- **Cost**: 625 Production
- **Tech Required**: Acoustics
- **Effects**:
  - +3 Gold
  - 3 Great Work of Art slots
  - Theming Bonus for works from Different Eras
  - +1 Great Artist Points

### Dégé Parkhang (Industrial Era)
- **Cost**: 750 Production
- **Tech Required**: Steam Power
- **Effects**:
  - Bonus Religious Pressure and Range per Great Work of Writing in the City
  - 2 Great Work of Writing slots
  - +3 Great Writer Points

## Installation

1. Download the mod files
2. Extract to your Civilization V Mods folder:
   - **Windows**: `Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization 5\MODS\`
   - **Mac**: `~/Documents/Aspyr/Sid Meier's Civilization 5/MODS/`
   - **Linux**: `~/.local/share/Aspyr/Sid Meier's Civilization 5/MODS/`
3. Launch Civilization V
4. Go to Mods menu
5. Enable "Tibetan Wonder Pack"
6. Start a new game

## Compatibility

- **Civilization V**: Base game + Gods & Kings + Brave New World required
- **Save Games**: This mod affects saved games. Existing saves without the mod cannot load it mid-game
- **Multiplayer**: All players must have the mod installed
- **Other Mods**: Generally compatible with most mods. May conflict with other wonder packs or mods that heavily modify the wonder system

## Known Issues

- Art assets are not included and must be added separately (see Art Requirements)
- Some complex mechanics may need balancing based on player feedback
- Multiplayer synchronization for custom mechanics needs testing

## Art Requirements

This mod requires custom art assets to display properly. See `Art/README_ART_REQUIREMENTS.md` for a complete list of needed files. The mod will function without art but wonders will display with placeholder icons.

## Historical Context

Each wonder is based on real historical sites and institutions:

- **Yumbu Lagang**: Legendary first building in Tibet, seat of early Tibetan kings
- **Jokhang Temple**: Most sacred temple in Tibetan Buddhism, built in 7th century CE
- **Samye Monastery**: First Buddhist monastery in Tibet, established around 779 CE
- **Sakya Library**: Contains one of the world's largest collections of Tibetan manuscripts
- **Potala Palace**: Former residence of the Dalai Lama, iconic symbol of Tibet
- **Norbulingka**: "Jewel Park," summer palace with spectacular gardens
- **Gyantse Kumbum**: Unique multi-story stupa containing 75 chapels
- **Dégé Parkhang**: Tibet's most important printing house, preserving Buddhist texts
